# Idea template

Use this layout for the file that `brainstorm` writes. Leave out a section that has no content. Write for a reader who was not in the session.

```markdown
# <Idea name> idea

Status: outcome of a brainstorm on <date>. It says what to build and why, and leaves how to the design. Nothing is built. The `refine` skill turns this file into `design.md`.

## The idea

<Two sentences on what it is and who uses it. The owner has confirmed this wording.>

## Who it is for

<The person, the situation they are in, and what they use or do today instead.>

## Why it should exist

<The closest existing products, where each one stops, and the gap this idea fills.>

## The map

<The final map as a nested list, with the idea at the root. Mark a branch that came from the agent with "(agent's suggestion)".>

- **Centre:** <the one thing it must do well>
- **Does**
  - <a thing it lets the person do>
  - <another>
- **Not**
  - <what it leaves out or refuses to become>
- **Good means:** <what would make it good, and not only working>

## In the owner's words

<What the owner said they want and care about, close to their own phrasing. Include the constraints they stated, such as "few solid features".>

## What exists

For each product the session looked at, write what it does about this problem and where you read it.

**<Product>** (<repository or site>)

- <what it does, and where it stops short of this idea>
- Source: <file, doc page or URL>. <"Read directly", "secondary source" or "from memory, not checked">

## Turned down

<Suggestions and versions of the idea that the owner rejected, each with their reason. The design should not bring these back without a new fact.>

## For the design

<Questions about how it works that came up and were set aside. The `refine` skill starts here.>

## Not verified

<Claims the idea depends on that nobody has tested or looked up. Write one line for each, with what would settle it.>

## Still unclear

<Branches of the map that were still unclear when the session ended. Leave this section out when the owner confirmed the whole map.>
```
